Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms need to stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Topeka HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, proper tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service built around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with written service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits your operating schedule. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, keeping the kitchen running and the dining room comfortable throughout.

Inspections & Documentation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify correct venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a documentation binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

Fast Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Topeka HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Topeka and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

Our technicians are equipped to handle any commercial HVAC emergency, including failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, and control board lockouts. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring heating or cooling as quickly as possible, then put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records and facility files.

Maintenance Programs

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every Topeka HVAC program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes using the right MERV rating for the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal prep for peak demand.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Our commercial clients consistently report fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills after implementing a program. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
